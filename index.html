Mingi Kim

I am an MS student at the VCLab, part of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Chungnam National University, where I work on 3D computer vision. My advisor is Prof. Hyungki Kim.

I have a BS in Mathematics and Computer Science (double major) from Chungnam National University. During my undergraduate studies, I worked as a research intern in the Nonlinear Analysis and PDEs Lab under the supervision of Prof. JinHae Park.

Research

My research interests lie in computer graphics, 3D vision, and multimodal large language models (MLLMs). In particular, I work on LLMs that understand and generate 3D point clouds and CAD data.
